Museums in Poznań: opening hours, prices and free days

36 museums from the guide in one table: when they are open, how much a ticket costs and on which days admission is free. The data comes from the attraction pages, so you can check every figure at its source (how we verify information, in Polish).

TableOpen on MondayFree admission

Open on Monday

On Mondays most museums are closed. Below are only those where our sources state explicitly that they are open on Mondays. If a museum has no Monday hours in our data, we do not put it on this list.

  • Chocolate Museum
  • MoJA Jewellery Museum
  • Poznań Fotoplastikon
  • St Martin's Croissant Museum

Free admission by day

  • Tuesday: Archaeological Museum, Armament Museum, Fort VII – Museum of the Martyrdom of Greater Poland People, Greater Poland Military Museum, Museum of Decorative Arts, Museum of Musical Instruments, Museum of the Greater Poland Uprising, Museum of World Cultures, National Museum, “Poznań” Army Museum
  • Thursday: Armoured Weapons Museum
  • Saturday: Henryk Sienkiewicz Literary Museum
  • Sunday: Museum of the Poznań June 1956
  • Always free: Earth Museum of Adam Mickiewicz University, Museum of Pharmacy, Poznań University of Medical Sciences Museum, PRL Room in the former Hall W7

Free with the Poznań Tourist Card: Greater Poland Military Museum, Museum of Musical Instruments.
