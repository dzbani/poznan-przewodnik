Models of Old Poznań

In the crypt of the Franciscan church on ul. Ludgardy stand detailed models of historic Poznań, presented with commentary, light and sound.

  • Hours see below
  • Tickets see below

What to see

A model of old Poznań at a scale of 1:150, reconstructed from an engraving of 1618. It has more than 2,300 objects and was the first such detailed historical model in Poland.

A model of the Stronghold of the First Piasts and a model of the Old Market Square.

One of the models, at a scale of 1:100, has tactile elements and Braille descriptions for blind visitors.

Good to know

Shows start at 11:00, 11:45, 12:30, 13:15, 14:00, 14:45, 15:30, 16:15 and 17:00. From 1 October to 31 March the last show starts at 15:30.

You can buy tickets up to 15 minutes before a show, or in advance. Payment is by cash or BLIK.

With the Poznań Tourist Card a ticket is 25% cheaper.
