Museum of World Cultures

The Museum of World Cultures is the former Ethnographic Museum, a branch of the National Museum. It received its new name in 2025. It is housed in a late neoclassical building of 1817–1819, the former seat of a Masonic lodge.

  • Hours Monday: closed · Tuesday–Wednesday: 10:00–16:00 · Thursday: 10:00–18:00 · Friday: 10:00–20:00 · Saturday–Sunday: 10:00–17:00
  • Tickets Standard: PLN 20 · Reduced: PLN 13 · Children and young people aged 8–26: PLN 1 · Tuesday: free admission

What to see

Collections of Polish and Greater Poland culture and of cultures from distant regions of the world, shown in temporary exhibitions.

Good to know

The entrance to the museum is from ul. Mostowa 7, through the park.

The museum organises guided tours, lessons, workshops, concerts and weekend meetings.
