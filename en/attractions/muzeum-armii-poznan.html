“Poznań” Army Museum

The “Poznań” Army Museum is housed in the casemates of Fort Winiary in Cytadela Park. It tells the story of the “Poznań” Army, commanded in 1939 by Gen. Tadeusz Kutrzeba, and of the army of the Second Polish Republic in Greater Poland. It is a branch of the Greater Poland Independence Museum.

  • Hours Monday: closed · Tuesday–Sunday: 10:00–16:00 · Last entry: 15:30
  • Tickets Standard: PLN 10 · Reduced: PLN 6 · Combined ticket with the Armament Museum: PLN 20, reduced PLN 12 · Tuesday: free admission

What to see

The permanent exhibition “Faithful to the Oath” presents the combat trail of the “Poznań” Army.

The collections include documents, banners, weapons, uniforms and iconographic material on the Polish Army of the interwar period, especially Greater Poland units. A collection of photographs of everyday life and service in Greater Poland garrisons stands out.

An important source is a large-format plan of Poznań from 1932 with military buildings and land marked.

About the place

The museum occupies a casemate corridor called the Small Lock (Mała Śluza). Originally it was the Western Viaduct of Fort Winiary, which linked it with Fort St Adalbert (Haka).

In 1974 the first of fourteen plaques commemorating units and formations of the “Poznań” Army was unveiled on the counterscarp wall. The permanent exhibition was opened on 31 August 1982.

Good to know

Since 20 January 2026 the museum has been open from Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–16:00. The last entry to the exhibition is at 15:30.

The Armament Museum operates in the same park. A combined ticket for both museums costs PLN 20 (reduced PLN 12). On Tuesdays admission is free. A guided tour in Polish costs PLN 30 (WMN price list), and it is worth arranging a date with the museum. Tickets can also be bought online at tobilet.pl.

Getting there (according to the ZTM timetable of 10 October 2026): trams 4 and 19 (stop Murawa) and 3, 4, 10 and 19 (stop Pasieka), or buses to the stops Urząd Marszałkowski, Winogrady, Armii Poznań and Grochowe Łąki. Free parking is by the park, with entry from ul. Garbary.
