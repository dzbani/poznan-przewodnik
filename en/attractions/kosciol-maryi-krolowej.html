Church of Mary Queen

The church was built in 1904–1907 in the Neo-Renaissance style. Until the end of the Second World War it belonged to the Protestant congregation as the church of St Matthew. Since 1945 it has been Catholic.

History

After it was taken over by the Catholic parish its dedication was changed to the present one.

What to see

A tower 45 metres high with an entrance portal, on the axis of the building.

An interior with a barrel vault, stained glass, wall paintings and sculptures.

A monument to the Archangel Michael in front of the church.

Good to know

This is a working church. It cannot be visited during Mass and services, and you are asked to dress modestly and keep quiet.
