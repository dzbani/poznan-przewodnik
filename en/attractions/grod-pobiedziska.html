Pobiedziska Stronghold

The Pobiedziska Stronghold is a family amusement park in which children and adults learn about life in the early Middle Ages. It offers an arena for foam-sword fights, archery and crossbow shooting and a catapult, a hire shop for medieval costumes and a campfire with medieval cooking.

  • Hours see below
  • Tickets Standard: PLN 20 · Reduced: PLN 15 · Family 2+2: PLN 60 · Family 2+3: PLN 65 · Children under 3: free · Catapult shot: PLN 10

Important before you go

For individual visitors the Stronghold is open from May to September, on Saturdays and Sundays and every day in July and August. From October groups can visit only by prior booking (until the end of October).

What to do

Foam-sword fights in a special arena, shooting with a bow, a crossbow and a catapult.

Hire of medieval costumes and a game of catching wooden fish in a pond.

A campfire with shows of historical cooking and a souvenir shop.

Good to know

There is a covered shelter on the grounds, and in bad weather you can use the educational room. Parking at the site is free.

The Stronghold accepts organised groups from April to October by prior booking. Its website honours the Poznań Tourist Card and the Large Family Card.

A catapult shot costs PLN 10, and a campfire PLN 150. Organised groups are accepted from April to October, 8:00–20:00, by prior booking (601 377 802 or biuro@grodpobiedziska.pl).

Getting there from Poznań

About 25 km from the centre of Poznań as the crow flies.

Train. Pobiedziska lies on the Poznań–Gniezno line, at the station Pobiedziska Letnisko.

Car. Pobiedziska is halfway between Poznań and Gniezno, on road no. 194. Parking is free.
