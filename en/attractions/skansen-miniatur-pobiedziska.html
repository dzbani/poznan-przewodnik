Piast Trail Miniature Park in Pobiedziska

The Miniature Park in Pobiedziska shows models of important and lesser-known buildings of the Piast Trail and Greater Poland at a scale of 1:20. It lies on the route from Poznań to Gniezno and is open all year. In 2026 it received the title of Recommended Tourist Attraction of Poznań County.

  • Hours April–September: 10:00–18:00 · October–March: 9:00–16:00
  • Tickets see below

What to see

More than 30 miniatures, among them Rogalin Palace, Gniezno Cathedral, the Poznań Opera and St Martin's Church in Poznań. It is a good way to get to know the region's monuments before a trip, or to see them from a child's perspective.

A hundred metres away stands the Pobiedziska Stronghold, so the two places are easy to combine in one visit.

Good to know

Groups of more than 10 people pay PLN 10 (adults) and PLN 8 (children), and one carer for every 15 children enters free. A guided tour costs PLN 13 (adults) and PLN 11 (children).

Visitors to the Pobiedziska Stronghold also use the toilets in the Miniature Park.

Getting there from Poznań

About 25 km from the centre of Poznań as the crow flies.

Train. Pobiedziska lies on the Poznań–Gniezno line, at the station Pobiedziska Letnisko (information from the Pobiedziska Stronghold website).

Car. Pobiedziska is halfway between Poznań and Gniezno, on road no. 194. Parking at the stronghold is free.
