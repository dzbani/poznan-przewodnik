Museum of Armoured Weapons

The Museum of Armoured Weapons is a branch of the Polish Army Museum in Warsaw. It was established on 1 April 2015 and has operated in its present home on ul. 3 Pułku Lotniczego since October 2019.

What to see

About 230 exhibits, including 75 vehicles from around the world.

The exhibition fills four pavilions of 700 m² each and a covered open-air exhibition of 3,000 m².

The displays tell of the Greater Poland Uprising, the Second World War, Gen. Stanisław Maczek's 1st Armoured Division, a hundred years of Polish armoured forces and the Cold War.

Good to know

Allow at least 2 hours for your visit.

On Thursdays admission to the permanent exhibition is free. The free ticket is collected at the ticket office.

The ticket office is open until 17:00, which is also the time of the last entry. Tickets can also be bought through the Bilety24 service.
