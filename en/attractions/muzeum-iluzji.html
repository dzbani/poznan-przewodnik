Museum of Illusions, Science and Art

The Museum of Illusions, Science and Art is on ul. Woźna, right next to the Old Market Square. It combines optical illusions with science and art, and the exhibits can be touched and tried out.

  • Hours Monday: closed · Tuesday–Sunday: 10:00–19:00
  • Tickets Ticket prices: at muzeumins.pl

What to see

More than 60 exhibits in seven exhibition spaces: optical illusions, puzzles and large installations.

An upside-down room, a Pixel Table and stations where you can “levitate”.

Good to know

The last entry is at 17:30.

Current ticket prices are given by the museum on its website and on social media.
