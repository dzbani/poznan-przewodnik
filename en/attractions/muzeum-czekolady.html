Chocolate Museum

The Chocolate Museum on ul. Wrocławska, a few steps from the Old Market Square, combines a visit with workshops. A visit lasts about 50 minutes.

  • Hours Daily: 10:00–18:00 · Saturday: until 19:00 · Sunday: from 11:00
  • Tickets Visit with a workshop: PLN 65 per person (children and adults)

What happens here

A presentation on the history of chocolate “from bean to bar” and a film on growing cocoa.

A tasting of cocoa and Belgian chocolate.

Making pralines and your own bar of chocolate yourself, which you take home.

Good to know

You do not need to book. Entries are on the hour.

Workshops are run in Polish and English.

During school-term weeks the museum receives individual visitors between 13:00 and 18:00.
