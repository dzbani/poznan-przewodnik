Church of the Resurrection of the Lord

The church was built in 1923–1926. Its neoclassical form was meant to express gratitude for the regaining of independence in 1918 and to commemorate the 950th anniversary of the baptism of Poland. It has been run by the Resurrectionists from the beginning.

  • Hours Visiting: outside services · Mass times: in the parish notices
  • Tickets Admission: free

History

The architecture and decoration refer to the symbolism of rebirth and victory.

What to see

A monumental portico with Ionic columns, recalling a Greek temple.

A single-aisled hall interior with a coffered ceiling and a statue of the victorious Christ in the chancel.

A tower with figures of Polish saints: Andrew Bobola, Casimir, Stanislaus of Szczepanów and John Cantius.

Good to know

This is a working church. It cannot be visited during Mass and services, and you are asked to dress modestly and keep quiet.
